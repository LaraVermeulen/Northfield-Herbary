Our origin story

Northfield Herbary began in a small kitchen garden at the edge of a pine forest. We grew what the seasons gave us – chamomile in June, berries in late summer, ginger dried by the stove in winter – and steeped it for friends who needed an evening to slow down.

Today, Northfield Herbary carries that same garden in every blend. No rush, no noise. Just leaves, warm water and a moment that belongs to you.
